# Inverted Sugar Syrup

**Section:** Basics

MAKES 5 CUPS
Granulated sugar
1000g | 4 cups
Water
500g | 2 cups
Citric or malic acid, or cream of tartar
5g | 1 teaspoon (or 1 tablespoon lemon juice)

**Cook the sugar.** Place the sugar, water, and acid in a medium pot set over
high heat. Cook, stirring to help dissolve the sugar, until the syrup is clear
and has come to a boil. Reduce the heat to a simmer, and continue cooking
the sugar syrup until a kitchen thermometer reads 245°F.

**Cool and store.** Remove the pot from heat, and let the syrup cool at room
temperature for 1 hour. Transfer it to an airtight container with a lid, and
store at room temperature for up to 6 months.
