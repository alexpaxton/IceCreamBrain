# Rhubarb Puree

**Section:** Basics

MAKES ABOUT 2 CUPS
Rhubarb
450g | 1 pound (4 to 8 stalks)

**Clean and prep.** Rinse the rhubarb stalks, using your hands to remove any
dirt that remains. Chop the stems into 1-inch pieces.

**Cook.** Place the rhubarb in a medium heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium
heat. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally with a spoon to help mash the
rhubarb, until it is bubbling. Uncover the pot, reduce the heat to low, and
simmer the fruit for 5 to 10 minutes, until it has released all its juices.
Continue mashing the fruit, as necessary.

**Puree.** Transfer the rhubarb to a blender and let cool for 10 minutes. Blend
on medium speed until it starts to break down, then increase the speed to high
and liquefy it. Stop the blender to press the fruit to the bottom of the cup, if
necessary.

**Strain and store.** Pass the puree through a fine-mesh sieve. Store in an
airtight container in the refrigerator for 1 week, or in the freezer for 3
months.
