# Cheesecake Neapolitan

**Section:** Composed Scoops



Makes between 3 and 3½ quarts ice cream
My friend Jason grew up thinking Neapolitan ice cream was a variety pack,
each of his siblings choosing their favorite—chocolate, strawberry, or
vanilla—from the carton. Cheesecake Neapolitan swirls the flavors together,
replacing the simple vanilla with cheesecake ice cream. With one taste,
you’ll believe that these three were born to be together.
1 batch Cheesecake Ice Cream, just churned
1 batch Blue Ribbon Chocolate Ice Cream, hardened
1 batch Strawberry Sherbet, hardened
Follow the method for swirling flavors on this page.
