# Mint Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough

**Section:** Composed Scoops



Makes between 1½ and 2 quarts ice cream
My friend Caroline notoriously struggles with making decisions, and thank
goodness she does. This mash-up flavor was created in a moment of her
indecision when I pressed her for her favorite flavor. “I don’t know! Mint
Chocolate Chip? Cookie Dough?” she asked me. I simply replied, “Yes,” and
the two of us have never looked back.
150g | 1 cup Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough pieces
80g | ½ cup Classic Chocolate Chunks
1 batch Garden Mint Ice Cream
Follow the method for layering bits on this page.
