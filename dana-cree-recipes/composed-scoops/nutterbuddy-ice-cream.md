# Nutterbuddy Ice Cream

**Section:** Composed Scoops



Makes between 1½ and 2 quarts ice cream
I prepare Nutterbuddies—small crispy peanut-butter-and-chocolate squares
—as a confection for many of the restaurants I’ve worked for, in Seattle and
Chicago, and they’ve garnered quite a cult following. This ice cream was
created for the legions of Nutterbuddy fans, and has earned a few more fans
of its own along the way.
150g | ¾ cup Creamy Caramel Ribbon, stirred
200g | 1½ cups Nutterbuddy Crunch
1 batch Vanilla Ice Cream, just churned
Follow the method for layering bits and ripples on this page.
