# Strawberry Bubblemallow

**Section:** Composed Scoops



Makes between 1½ and 2 quarts ice cream
Bubblegum Ice Cream is the perfect setting for a ribbon of strawberry and
big fluffy pieces of marshmallow. Anyone who loved bubblegum as a kid
will find this suits their inner child and their adult taste buds at the same
time. And it’s a perfect ice cream to make for any little ones running around
your life!
150g | ¾ cup Strawberry Ripple
100g | 2 cups Marshmallows
1 batch Bubblegum Ice Cream, just churned
Follow the method for layering bits and ripples on this page.
