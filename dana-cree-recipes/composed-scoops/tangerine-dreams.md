# Tangerine Dreams

**Section:** Composed Scoops



Makes between 1½ and 2 quarts sherbet
This sherbet is perfect for curing the winter blues. When the holidays are
over and summer is barely a light at the end of the tunnel, citrus fruits are in
the peak of their season. The Blood Orange Sherbet gets a facelift by
substituting tangy tangerines. A bright passion fruit caramel winds its way
throughout the ice cream between tiny wheels of candied kumquats, while
crispy bits of Roasted-Vanilla Meringue offer sweet refuge from the bright
flavors, and a little texture as well.
150g | ¾ cup Passion Fruit–Orange Caramel
100g | ½ cup Candied Citrus Bits, made with kumquat, sliced
30g | 1 cup Roasted-Vanilla Meringue Bits
1 batch Blood Orange Sherbet made with tangerines instead, just
churned
Follow the method for layering bits and ripples on this page.
