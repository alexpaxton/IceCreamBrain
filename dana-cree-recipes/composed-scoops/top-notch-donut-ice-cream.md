# Top-Notch Donut Ice Cream

**Section:** Composed Scoops



Makes between 1½ and 2 quarts ice cream
Donut fanatics: this ice cream was created especially for you. Swirling the
fudge ripple and sprinkles into this ice cream mimics the chocolate glaze and
sprinkles of one of my favorite donuts, but don’t stop there! You can replicate
any of your favorite donuts by, for example, adding Lemon Curd or raspberry
jam, or sprinkling in coconut flakes or chocolate jimmies!
150g | ¾ cup Fudge Ripple
15g | 3 tablespoons sprinkles
1 batch Donut Ice Cream, just churned
Follow the method for layering bits and ripples on this page.
