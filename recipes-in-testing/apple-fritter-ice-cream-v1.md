# Apple Fritter Ice Cream v1

The base for an apple fritter ice cream, built to scoop at 5°F / -15°C while reading only
slightly sweet. Those two goals normally fight each other, and allulose is what resolves it:
at 190 PAC and 0.7x sucrose sweetness it delivers 271 units of freezing point depression per
unit of perceived sweetness, against sucrose's 100. Two thirds of the fat is refined coconut
oil emulsified into skim milk with sunflower lecithin; the remaining third is butterfat from
cream, present because coconut oil melts across too narrow a band to read as creamy alone.
Skim milk powder carries the protein and total solids a Philadelphia base has no egg yolks to
provide. Salt runs high at 0.22% to push the fritter's sweet-savory, fried-dough direction.
Flavor comes from a 4-day cold infusion of apple fritter donut in the skim milk, strained
out before the base is built.

## Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient |
|-----|-----------|
| 1040g | Fat-free (skim) milk, infused and strained *(yield weight, not starting weight)* |
| 1.5 whole | Apple fritter donut, torn into pieces *(infusion only, strained out; not weighed, roughly 150-210g)* |
| 151g | Heavy cream |
| 47g | Skim milk powder |
| 105.5g | Refined coconut oil |
| 6.5g | Sunflower lecithin |
| 176.5g | Allulose |
| 50g | Sucrose |
| 15.8g | Dextrose |
| 3.5g | Salt |
| 2.4g | Guar gum |
| 1.6g | Xanthan gum |

**Totals:** 1599.8g · TS 34.9% · Fat 10.0% (66% coconut / 33% butterfat) · MSNF 9.1% ·
Protein 3.5% · Salt 0.22% · Lactose 7.7% of water phase · PAC 32.1 · FP -3.55°C / 25.6°F ·
POD ~12.4

Est. serving temp -16.05°C / 3.1°F. The added sucrose and salt pushed PAC from 29.9 to 32.1,
so at 5°F / -15°C this now sits slightly softer than the original target rather than exactly
on it. Salt alone contributes 1.28 of that (PAC 585, the highest value in the table).

**All figures above exclude the donut infusion.** The donut leaves behind sugar, salt, and
fryer oil in quantities that cannot be measured, so they are deliberately not modeled. Every
one of those pushes the same direction: sugar and salt raise PAC (softer, lower freezing
point), and the oil raises fat while diluting the 66% coconut share. Treat the numbers as a
floor, not a value. Actual serving texture at 5°F / -15°C will be softer than -16.05°C
predicts.

## Tools

- Vitamix 5500 blender
- Stainless steel saucier pan
- Instant-read thermometer
- Milligram precision scale
- Food scale
- Metal mesh strainer
- Stainless steel mixing bowls
- Silicone spatula
- Whynter 2.1 Qt. ice cream maker

## Procedure

**Infuse the milk.** Tear 1.5 apple fritters into pieces, submerge in skim milk, and hold at 4°C / 39°F for 4 days. Strain through a superbag, wringing hard to recover as much milk as possible, then discard the solids. **Start with more milk than 1040g.** Fried dough holds several times its own weight and the 1040g here is the post-strain yield, not the pour. The starting weight was not recorded in v1; measure both ends next time so the absorption ratio is known and the yield becomes predictable instead of discovered.

**Disperse the gums.** Whisk the guar gum and xanthan gum into the allulose, sucrose, dextrose, and salt while all are dry. Gums hydrate on contact and will seize into permanent lumps if they hit liquid loose. Set aside.

**Hydrate the milk powder cold.** Combine the infused skim milk and heavy cream in the Vitamix, add the skim milk powder, and blend on low 30 seconds until no dry pockets remain. Milk powder disperses in cold liquid and clumps in hot.

**Heat.** Transfer to the saucier and bring to 75°C / 167°F. Hold 10 minutes. With no egg yolks, denatured whey protein is the primary structural asset, and this hold is where it comes from.

**Hydrate the sugars and gums.** Whisk the dry mixture into the hot base. Hold at 75°C / 167°F for 5 more minutes. Guar only reaches full viscosity hot.

**Melt the oil.** Warm the refined coconut oil to 40-50°C / 104-122°F, fully liquid and clear.

**Homogenize in two passes.** 1600g will not clear the Vitamix's working line in one go. Split the hot base in half, and split the lecithin and melted oil in half with it. Per pass: blend base and lecithin on low 2 minutes, then stream in the oil slowly while still on low, then ramp to high for 45 seconds until fully opaque. Keep everything above 30°C / 86°F throughout — coconut oil sets at 24°C / 76°F and will break the emulsion into grain if the mixture cools mid-blend. Recombine both passes after.

**Strain and chill.** Pass through the mesh strainer, then chill over an ice bath.

**Age 8-12 hours at 4°C / 39°F.** Do not skip. The coconut oil has to crystallize before churning or the fat network cannot partially coalesce during agitation and the base will churn to foam with no body.

**Churn cold, in two batches.** Weigh out ~800g per churn — 1600g of base at 25-30% overrun is ~2.0L against a 1.99L bowl, and an overfilled bowl churns slowly, which grows larger ice crystals. The aged base will be thick and close to set. Do not re-blend, that shears the emulsion apart. Churn straight from the fridge and hold the second half refrigerated until the machine is free.

## What to watch on the first test

- **Waxy or coating finish** → coconut share too high; shift toward cream.
- **Gritty or sandy after a few days in the freezer** → lactose crystallizing; drop milk powder to ~31g.
- **Slimy or gluey rather than creamy** → guar/xanthan synergy overshooting; cut the xanthan to 0.8g before touching the guar.
- **Too firm at 5°F / -15°C** → add allulose in 16g steps, roughly +1.9 PAC each.
- **Soupy or won't set up** → sweetener is over; pull allulose, not sucrose.
- **Reads salty rather than seasoned** → 0.22% is above the 0.1-0.15% norm; drop to 2.4g. Note the infusion also carries salt in, so the effective figure is above 0.22%.
- **Softer than expected at 5°F / -15°C** → expected. The unquantified infusion sugar and salt both add PAC on top of the modeled 32.1. Pull allulose in v2 to compensate.
- **Fritter flavor too faint** → 4 days is already long for a cold dairy infusion. Increase fritter quantity rather than time; past ~4 days you gain stale and rancid fryer-oil notes faster than apple and cinnamon.

## For v2

Record these three numbers, none of which exist for v1:

1. Fritter weight in grams, not count. Shop fritters vary widely and "1.5" is not reproducible.
2. Skim milk starting weight before infusion.
3. Skim milk yield weight after wringing.

(2) minus (3) gives the absorption, and absorption divided by (1) gives a ratio that makes the yield predictable on every later batch. Without those, v2 cannot be a controlled change from v1, only a second guess.
