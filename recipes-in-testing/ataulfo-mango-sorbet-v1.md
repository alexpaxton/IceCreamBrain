# Ataulfo Mango Sorbet v1

Pure ataulfo mango sorbet at 60% sweetness. The 3884g of ripe mango is the entire base — its natural water, sugars, and solids — with no added liquid or dairy. A 60/40 sucrose/glucose split keeps sweetness present without cloying and softens the freeze. The dominant aroma compounds are monoterpene terpenes (δ-3-carene, terpinolene) and the fruity ester ethyl butanoate (BP 120°C), all of which volatilize above ~60°C — the puree is never heated. Batch yields ~4.5L; plan for 3 churn passes in the Whynter.

## Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient |
|-----|-----------|
| 3884 | Ripe ataulfo mango, peeled and pitted |
| 387 | Sucrose |
| 258 | Glucose syrup |
| 5 | Xanthan gum |

> FPD index: 1.36 (added sweeteners only) — mango's natural fructose/glucose pushes combined FPD to ~1.40 across all sugars; in range for sorbet.

## Tools

- Chef knife
- Food scale
- Vitamix 5500 blender
- Tamis (60-mesh)
- Stainless steel mixing bowls
- Magnetic stirring plate
- Instant-read thermometer
- Whynter ice cream maker

## Procedure

**Purée.** Peel and pit mangoes; blend in Vitamix in batches until smooth. Keep puree cold throughout — do not apply any heat.

**Strain.** Pass puree through the 60-mesh tamis to remove fibers.

**Dissolve sugars.** Whisk glucose syrup into cold puree until incorporated. Add sucrose and place bowl on the magnetic stirring plate; stir at room temperature (~20°C / 68°F) until fully dissolved, about 5 minutes. If sucrose resists, use a warm water bath briefly — keep puree below 40°C / 104°F.

**Stabilize.** Whisk xanthan gum into 50g of puree in a small bowl until lump-free, then stir into the full batch.

**Chill.** Refrigerate at 4°C / 39°F for at least 2 hours.

**Churn.** Churn in ~1.5L batches in the Whynter to leave room for expansion.
