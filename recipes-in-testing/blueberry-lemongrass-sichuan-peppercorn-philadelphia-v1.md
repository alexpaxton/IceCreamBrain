# Blueberry Lemongrass Sichuan Peppercorn Philadelphia v1

An eggless base with blueberry in two forms. A cooked-down purée goes into the base for depth and color. A raw purée is swirled in after churning for fresh berry flavor. Lemongrass and red Sichuan peppercorn steep cold in the cream. All their main aroma compounds and the tingling compound (hydroxy-α-sanshool) dissolve in fat. Linalool is in all three ingredients: it is the main aroma in Sichuan peppercorn, a key aroma in blueberry, and a minor note in lemongrass. Myrcene and limonene connect lemongrass and Sichuan peppercorn to the blueberry terpenes. Thus the three flavors share a floral, citrus core. The tingle is the contrast. The steep stays cold and no peppercorns are toasted. This keeps the floral notes and prevents the fried-oil note (2,4-decadienal) that forms at 130°C (266°F) and above. Blueberry is acidic. It goes in only after the cream is strained and cold, so the acid cannot change the lemongrass citral into off-flavors or break down the sanshool. Added sugar is 41% sucrose and 59% allulose by weight. This keeps the sweetness of a 50% sucrose/dextrose split and increases freezing power to keep the ice cream scoopable at -15°C (5°F). A 1:1 guar and xanthan blend gives body and slows ice crystal growth.

## Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient |

| 100g | Allulose |
| 1g | Salt |
| 0.75g | Xanthan gum |
| 0.75g | Guar gum |

| 40g | Lemongrass, ~2 stalks *(infusion only, strained out)* |
| 5g | Red Sichuan peppercorn *(infusion only, strained out)* |

> FPD index: 1.53 (in range)

**Raw blueberry swirl (added after churning, not part of the base)**

| Qty | Ingredient |

| 150g | Blueberries, raw |

- Stainless steel pots

- Food scale
- Milligram precision scale
- Vitamix 5500 blender

- Instant-read thermometer
- Whynter 2.1 Qt. ice cream maker

## Procedure

**Cold steep the cream.** Remove the black seeds and twigs from the Sichuan peppercorn. Crack the husks lightly in the mortar. Do not grind them to powder. Bruise the lemongrass hard along its length with the rolling pin, then cut it into 5 cm (2 in) pieces. Put both in the 360g cream and hold at 4°C (39°F) or below for 24 hours.

**Strain the cream.** Pour through a fine mesh strainer and press the solids with a spatula. Top up with fresh cold cream to 360g.

**Cook down the purée.** Weigh the empty saucier. Put in 300g blueberries and mash them. Simmer over medium heat and stir frequently until the contents weigh 150g, about 15 to 20 minutes. Push the purée through the tamis to remove the skins. Chill to 4°C (39°F) or below.

**Make the raw swirl.** Blend 150g raw blueberries, 11g sucrose, and 15.5g allulose in the Vitamix until smooth. Push through the tamis. Refrigerate.

**Hydrate the gums.** Whisk 0.75g xanthan and 0.75g guar into 20g of the sucrose, taken from the 69g. This keeps the gums from clumping. Whisk the mixture into the 280g milk. Heat to 80°C (176°F), hold 2 minutes, then chill to 4°C (39°F) or below. Guar reaches full thickness only when heated.

**Build the base.** Immersion blend the nonfat dry milk, the remaining 49g sucrose, 100g allulose, and salt into the chilled gum milk. Add the strained cream. Add the cold purée last.

**Swirl after churning.** Put alternate layers of churned base and raw swirl in the container, 3 layers of each. Pull a spatula through once. Do not stir. The swirl freezes harder than the base at -15°C (5°F).
