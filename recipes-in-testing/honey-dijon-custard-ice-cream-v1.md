# Honey Dijon Custard Ice Cream v1

A rich egg-yolk custard sweetened entirely with honey, with Grey Poupon Dijon
mustard whisked in after cooling. Honey acts as a flavored invert sugar — its
fructose/glucose profile boosts FPD slightly above a sucrose baseline, so
expect a soft, slow-to-harden set. Dijon is added cold to preserve the sharp
allyl isothiocyanate bite; heat would mute it to a background earthiness.

## Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient |
|-----|-----------|
| 310g | Heavy cream |
| 370g | Whole milk |
| 75g | Egg yolks |
| 98g | Honey |
| 72g | Glucose syrup |
| 1g | Xanthan gum |
| 20g | Grey Poupon Dijon mustard |

> FPD index: 1.65 — slightly above range; texture will be soft.

## Tools

- Saucier pan
- Instant-read thermometer
- Milligram precision scale
- Metal mesh strainer
- Vitamix 5500 blender

## Procedure

**Bloom xanthan.** Whisk xanthan gum into the glucose syrup until fully
dispersed before combining with the base liquids. This prevents clumping.

**Cook custard.** Combine cream, milk, honey, and glucose-xanthan in the
saucier. Heat to 83°C, tempering into the egg yolks along the way. Do not
exceed 83°C — higher heat will degrade honey's floral aromatics. Strain.

**Chill and add mustard.** Cool the strained base over an ice bath to below
10°C. Whisk in Dijon mustard thoroughly, or blend briefly in the Vitamix for
full emulsification. Taste and adjust mustard quantity before churning.

**Churn.** Churn in the Whynter per standard procedure.
