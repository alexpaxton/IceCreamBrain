# Honey Dijon Custard Ice Cream v2

A rich egg-yolk custard sweetened with honey, glucose, and a small white sugar
addition. Honey provides flavor and invert-sugar softness; the white sugar trims
the FPD back toward a firmer set. Two mustards whisked in cold after chilling:
Dijon for sharp allyl isothiocyanate bite, French's yellow for a softer, more
vinegary mustard note underneath.

## Changes from v1

**Observations:** Previous batch set too soft and the mustard character was thin
— the single mustard addition at 20g read as background flavor rather than a
focal note.

**Adjustments:**
- Honey 98g → 130g — more sweetness and flavor body
- Dijon mustard 20g → 45g — significantly stronger mustard presence; brand dropped to generic
- Add 25g French's yellow mustard — second mustard layer, softer and more vinegary than dijon
- Add 20g white sugar — firms up set by reducing excess FPD from honey
- Removed FPD index note — no longer accurate with updated sweetener stack

## Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient |
|-----|-----------|
| 310g | Heavy cream |
| 370g | Whole milk |
| 75g | Egg yolks |
| 130g | Honey |
| 72g | Glucose syrup |
| 20g | White sugar |
| 45g | Dijon mustard |
| 25g | French's yellow mustard |
| 1g | Xanthan gum |

## Tools

- Saucier pan
- Instant-read thermometer
- Milligram precision scale
- Metal mesh strainer
- Vitamix 5500 blender

## Procedure

**Bloom xanthan.** Whisk xanthan gum into the glucose syrup until fully
dispersed before combining with the base liquids. This prevents clumping.

**Cook custard.** Combine cream, milk, honey, white sugar, and glucose-xanthan
in the saucier. Heat to 83°C (181°F), tempering into the egg yolks along the
way. Do not exceed 83°C — higher heat will degrade honey's floral aromatics.
Strain.

**Chill and add mustard.** Cool the strained base over an ice bath to below
10°C (50°F). Whisk in both mustards thoroughly, or blend briefly in the Vitamix
for full emulsification. Taste and adjust mustard quantities before churning.

**Churn.** Churn in the Whynter per standard procedure.
