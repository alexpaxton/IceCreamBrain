# Lime Lemongrass Makrut Fig Leaf Sherbet v2

Dairy lime sherbet built on Dana Cree's Blank Slate, with no buttermilk. Fresh lime juice supplies the tartness; the aroma comes from four steeped botanicals split across two sous vide bags so each runs at its own temperature. Every aroma compound here is fat-soluble (limonene and γ-terpinene in lime zest, citronellal in makrut leaf, geranial and neral in lemongrass, β-damascenone and the sesquiterpenes in fig leaf), so all steeping happens in the dairy before any acid goes in. Acid would cyclize citral and citronellal into off-flavors. Makrut and zest go low and short at 63°C / 145°F, where citronellal stays bright. Lemongrass and toasted fig leaf go hot at 80°C / 176°F: lemongrass is fibrous and its citral is heat-stable at neutral pH, and 80°C fully inactivates ficin, the milk-clotting protease in fig leaf sap, as a backup to the toast. Cream is high for a sherbet (12% fat). The heated whey proteins and the lime acid set the casein into a fine network that the fat fills, which gives a smooth, posset-like body; this is kept deliberately. Sweetener is 40/60 allulose/sucrose, balanced to PAC 30, the level the house recipes scoop well at when served at 5°F / -15°C.

## Changes from v1

**Observations:** Made at 1.2x scale with heavy cream raised to 393g and sweetener balanced to 80% water frozen at 5°F (480g, PAC 47.1, PACn 88.6, FPD -6.6°C / 20.1°F, 26.8% sucrose-equivalent). It churned 1.5 hours instead of 30–40 minutes, yielded about 1.5 qt (~3% overrun), stayed soft after 12 hours at -6°F / -21°C, and set like a posset. It was slightly too sweet; a pinch of malic acid balanced it. Short on zest, so extra makrut and lemongrass went in, and those two dominated.

**Adjustments:**
- Heavy cream 393g and whole milk 553.3g kept as made. Batch is now ~1325g instead of ~1000g.
- Sweetener cut from 480g to 229g (137.4g sucrose, 91.6g allulose), still 40/60. PAC 47.1 to 30.0, PACn 88.6 to 47.5, FPD -6.6°C / 20.1°F to -3.35°C / 26.0°F, sweetness 26.8% to 15.2% sucrose-equivalent. Balanced to PAC, not to % water frozen at 5°F.
- Salt 3.3g (2.5g/kg of the new batch).
- Gums 1.03g each (0.25% of ~824g water, 1:1), as made.
- Nonfat milk powder 24g and lime juice 120g, as made. Juice is now 9.1% of the batch.
- Malic acid added to taste at the end, optional.
- Posset-like set kept; boil, 80°C bag and acid unchanged.
- Botanical weights are starting points scaled to the larger batch, with zest restored as the main lime note. Final weights are set by eye and recorded below.

## Procedure

**Fig leaves.** Wear gloves when handling raw leaves; the sap contains psoralen, which burns skin under UV. Cut off the stems and wipe the leaves dry. Toast in the dry frying pan over medium heat, pressing flat and turning, until the whole leaf is crisp and snaps cleanly, center vein included. Any soft or flexible area may still hold active ficin. Target a coconut-vanilla smell and dull olive to light brown color. Black or acrid means burnt; discard and redo.

**Other botanicals.** Peel the zest with the paring knife, taking only the green layer (pith gives bitterness), then mince. Tear the makrut leaves and remove the center vein. Smash the lemongrass with the back of the chef knife, then slice 2–3mm thin.

**Gum premix.** Reserve 18g of the sucrose. Whisk the xanthan and guar into it until there are no streaks. Set aside dry.

**Boil the dairy.** Whisk the milk powder into the remaining sucrose and the allulose before adding to the cold milk and cream, so the powder does not clump. Add the salt. Bring to a boil and simmer 2 minutes.

**Cool and split.** Chill over an ice bath to 10°C / 50°F or below. A warm base boils under vacuum in the chamber sealer and loses volatiles before the seal closes. Weigh the base and divide it in half by weight.

**Bag.** Bag A: half the base, makrut leaves, zest. Bag B: half the base, lemongrass, toasted fig leaves. Seal both and flatten to about 2–3cm / 1in thick.

**Steep bag A.** 63°C / 145°F for 25–30 minutes, counted from when the bag reaches temperature (allow 10–15 minutes come-up). Makrut sets this time; past it, citronellal turns soapy and shifts to minty-medicinal isopulegol. Move the sealed bag to ice water.

**Steep bag B.** Raise the bath to 80°C / 176°F. Steep 30–45 minutes from when the bag reaches temperature. Do not exceed 45 minutes; fig leaf tannins set the ceiling. Move the sealed bag to ice water.

**Strain.** Chill both bags at least 10 minutes before opening, so the volatiles stay in the liquid. Strain both through the fine mesh into one bowl and press the botanicals. Weigh and restore to 1202.6g with whole milk.

**Gums.** With the base at or below 10°C / 50°F in the Vitamix on medium, rain in the gum premix and ramp to high for 30 seconds.

**Cure.** Hold at or below 4°C / 39°F at least 4 hours so the guar hydrates before it meets acid.

**Juice.** Squeeze the limes the same day. Just before churning, stream the juice into the cold base with the Vitamix on low so it disperses before casein can form visible curds. Taste, then add malic acid a pinch at a time if needed; dissolve it in a spoonful of the base first. Record the pH.

**Churn.** The mix is about 1.2L. Expect a churn time closer to the usual 30–40 minutes than v1's 1.5 hours.

**Harden and serve.** Harden at -6°F / -21°C, then hold and serve at 5°F / -15°C.

## Watch on test

- **Churn time and overrun.** v1 took 1.5 hours with ~3% overrun. Record both. The posset set and 12% fat may still keep overrun low even with normal freezing.
- **Texture.** With PAC 30 there is far more ice than v1. Note how much of the posset-like body remains.
- **Sweetness.** 15.2% sucrose-equivalent is at the middle of the house 14–16% range. With 12% fat and the lime acid, check that it does not read flat.
- **Botanical balance.** Zest should carry the lime note, with makrut and lemongrass behind it. Record the weights used.
- **Ficin.** Before the juice goes in, leave a small cup of strained base at room temperature for 30 minutes. Grain, thickening, or bitterness means ficin survived both the toast and the 80°C steep.
- **Fat.** At 12.1% fat, check for a waxy or coating mouthfeel at 5°F.
