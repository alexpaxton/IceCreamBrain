# Lime Sherbet v1

Dairy lime sherbet built on Dana Cree's Blank Slate, with no buttermilk. It is lime-lemongrass-makrut-fig-leaf-sherbet-v2 with the lemongrass, makrut and fig leaf removed, so lime is the only flavor. Lime juice supplies the tartness; zest supplies the aroma. The main zest compounds (limonene, γ-terpinene, β-pinene) are fat-soluble, so the zest steeps in the dairy in a sealed bag at 63°C / 145°F, where the sealed bag keeps the volatiles in and the temperature stays below where β-pinene and γ-terpinene start to degrade. Juice goes in only after straining, since acid breaks down the citral-type compounds in the peel. Cream is high for a sherbet (12% fat). The boiled whey proteins and the lime acid set the casein into a fine network that the fat fills, which gives a smooth, posset-like body. Sweetener is 40/60 allulose/sucrose, balanced to PAC 30.

- Stainless steel mixing bowls
- Metal mesh strainer (fine)
- Vitamix 5500 blender
- Instant-read thermometer
- Electronic pH meter
- Milligram precision scale
- Food scale (up to 15 lbs)
- Whynter 2.1 Qt. upright ice cream maker with built-in compressor

## Procedure

**Zest.** Peel with the paring knife, taking only the green layer (pith gives bitterness), then mince with the chef knife.

**Gum premix.** Reserve 18g of the sucrose. Whisk the xanthan and guar into it until there are no streaks. Set aside dry.

**Boil the dairy.** Whisk the milk powder into the remaining sucrose and the allulose before adding to the cold milk and cream, so the powder does not clump. Add the salt. Bring to a boil and simmer 2 minutes.

**Cool and bag.** Chill over an ice bath to 10°C / 50°F or below. A warm base boils under vacuum in the chamber sealer and loses volatiles before the seal closes. Bag the base with the zest, seal, and flatten to about 2–3cm / 1in thick. Use two bags if one will not lie flat at that thickness.

**Steep.** 63°C / 145°F for 25–30 minutes, counted from when the bag reaches temperature (allow 10–15 minutes come-up). Move the sealed bag to ice water.

**Strain.** Chill at least 10 minutes before opening, so the volatiles stay in the liquid. Strain through the fine mesh and press the zest. Weigh and restore to 1202.6g with whole milk.

**Gums.** With the base at or below 10°C / 50°F in the Vitamix on medium, rain in the gum premix and ramp to high for 30 seconds.

**Cure.** Hold at or below 4°C / 39°F at least 4 hours so the guar hydrates before it meets acid.

**Juice.** Squeeze the limes the same day. Just before churning, stream the juice into the cold base with the Vitamix on low so it disperses before casein can form visible curds. Taste, then add malic acid a pinch at a time if needed; dissolve it in a spoonful of the base first. Record the pH.

**Churn.** The mix is about 1.2L.

**Harden and serve.** Harden at -6°F / -21°C, then hold and serve at 5°F / -15°C.

## Watch on test

- **Churn time and overrun.** Record both. The posset set and 12% fat may keep overrun low.
- **Texture.** Note how much posset-like body there is at PAC 30. Without the 80°C bag, less whey protein is denatured than in the botanical version, so the set may be slightly weaker.
- **Lime strength.** Zest carries all the aroma. If it reads flat, raise zest weight. If bitter, the zest went too deep into the pith.
- **Sweetness.** 15.2% sucrose-equivalent, middle of the house 14–16% range.
- **Fat.** At 12.1% fat, check for a waxy or coating mouthfeel at 5°F.
