# Lychee Reàl & Jasmine Sorbet v1

Lychee Reàl syrup serves as both sweetener and primary flavoring — its concentrated lychee-puree base supplies cis-rose oxide, geraniol, and linalool, all of which are reinforced by jasmine tea's dominant compound, linalool, plus benzyl acetate and methyl anthranilate from the jasmine scenting. The shared linalool between the two ingredients makes this a genuinely complementary pairing rather than just additive. Lime juice adds acid contrast. Glucose syrup and xanthan gum together address the base's sucrose-dominant FPD and sorbet iciness.

## Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient |
|-----|-----------|
| 340g | Brewed jasmine tea, cooled |
| 500g | Lychee Reàl syrup |
| 75g | Fresh lime juice |
| 60g | Glucose syrup |
| 2g | Xanthan gum |
| 1.5g | Salt |

> FPD index: ~1.26 — in range. Sugar ~35% of base weight; sweetness is intentionally forward for this style.

## Tools

- Food scale
- Milligram precision scale
- Instant-read thermometer
- Stainless steel pot
- Metal mesh strainer
- Vitamix 5500
- Whynter ice cream maker

## Procedure

**Brew jasmine tea.** Heat water to 75–80°C. Steep at 5g tea per 100g water for 2–3 minutes, then strain immediately. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate. Do not re-heat.

**Combine.** Combine cooled jasmine tea, Lychee Reàl syrup, lime juice, glucose syrup, and salt. Weigh xanthan gum on milligram scale and blend into the mix in the Vitamix for 20–30 seconds to fully disperse without excess air.

**Chill.** Refrigerate until the base reaches ≤4°C, at least 2 hours.

**Churn.** Churn in Whynter to soft-serve consistency, transfer to container, freeze at least 3 hours before serving.
