# Nectarine Coconut Sherbet v2

Fruit-forward sherbet anchored by a large volume of nectarine puree. Cocojune coconut yogurt provides tang and light body — its live-culture fermented character complements stone fruit well. The light coconut milk fills out the base liquid without adding coconut richness. Allulose replaces glucose to boost PAC without adding sweetness, appropriate for sweet peak-season nectarines. Sunflower lecithin compensates for the low protein in both coconut products.

## Changes from v1

**Observations:** Flavor and texture were good, but nectarine presence was weaker than desired. Tested with +0.5g salt, +2g coconut oil, 10g honey added, and lemon juice omitted.

**Adjustments:**
- All non-nectarine ingredients reduced 15% from tested quantities to push nectarine proportion forward
- Honey (8.5g) carried forward from test as a permanent addition
- Lemon juice removed

## Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient |
|-----|-----------|
| 297.5g | Coconut milk (Costco/TCC) |
| 255g | Cocojune coconut yogurt |
| 963g | Nectarine puree |
| 110.5g | Sucrose |
| 158g | Allulose |
| 8.5g | Coconut oil |
| 8.5g | Honey |
| 5.1g | Sunflower lecithin |
| 1.7g | Xanthan gum |
| 2.6g | Salt |

## Tools

- Stainless steel saucier pan
- Instant-read thermometer
- Vitamix 5500 blender
- Milligram precision scale
- Whynter 2.1 Qt. ice cream maker

## Procedure

**Dissolve sugars.** Warm coconut milk in the saucier to 60°C / 140°F. Add coconut oil and whisk until melted. Whisk in sucrose, allulose, honey, and salt until fully dissolved. Cool to room temperature.

**Blend base.** Combine cooled coconut milk, coconut yogurt, and nectarine puree in the Vitamix. Stream in sunflower lecithin with the machine running on medium, then blend 30 seconds at high.

**Add xanthan.** With the Vitamix running on medium, add xanthan gum through the lid opening and immediately ramp to high. Blend 15 seconds.

**Chill and churn.** Chill to ≤4°C / 39°F, churn per Whynter instructions, then harden at least 2 hours.
