# Strawberry-Tomato Sorbet v1

A 50/50 blend of strawberry and tomato juice. Both juices run ~94% water and ~3% natural sugar,
so the sweetener load is high relative to most fruit sorbets — the ~36g of natural sugar from the
juices has been subtracted from the sweetener total. The tomato pulls toward savory and acidic;
the strawberry toward bright and fruity. This is an unusual combination worth testing at full
sweetness before considering adjustments.

## Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient |
|-----|-----------|
| 630 | Strawberry juice, 100% |
| 630 | Tomato juice, no salt added |
| 366 | Sucrose |
| 122 | Glucose / Dextrose |
| 3 | Salt |
| 2 | Xanthan gum |

> FPD index: 1.22 — in range

## Tools

- Whisk or immersion blender
- Ice cream machine

## Procedure

**Dissolve sugars.** Whisk sucrose, glucose, salt, and xanthan gum directly into the combined juices
at room temperature until fully dissolved. No heat required. Use no-salt-added tomato juice —
canned varieties with salt will read as noticeably salty once frozen.
