# Strawberry-Tomato Sorbet v2

Adjusted from v1 based on on-the-fly tasting. Sweetener split shifted heavily toward glucose
(46% sucrose / 54% glucose) for a softer freeze and less cloying sweetness. Salt increased to 5g
to reinforce the savory-tomato character. Xanthan reduced slightly to 1.5g.

## Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient |
|-----|-----------|
| 630 | Strawberry juice, 100% |
| 630 | Tomato juice, no salt added |
| 250 | Sucrose |
| 288 | Glucose / Dextrose |
| 5 | Salt |
| 1.5 | Xanthan gum |

> FPD index: 1.48 — in range

## Tools

- Whisk or immersion blender
- Ice cream machine

## Procedure

**Dissolve sugars.** Whisk sucrose, glucose, salt, and xanthan gum directly into the combined juices
at room temperature until fully dissolved. No heat required. Use no-salt-added tomato juice —
canned varieties with salt will read as noticeably salty once frozen.
