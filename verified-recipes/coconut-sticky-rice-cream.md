# Coconut Sticky Rice-Cream

A stripped-down test of a coconut non-dairy Philadelphia-style base, flavored with
steamed Thai sticky rice blended into the coconut milk and strained out through a
tamis. The rice contributes starchy sweetness and a subtle toasted grain note without
remaining in the final mix. Coconut oil is emulsified in post-strain with sunflower
lecithin standing in for egg yolk. No custard, no eggs.

## Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient |
|-----|-----------|
| 2970g | Aroy-D coconut milk (3 cartons) |
| 685g | Thai sticky rice, steamed *(infusion only — strained out)* |
| 242g | Coconut oil |
| 16g | Sunflower lecithin |
| 692g | Granulated white sugar |
| 346g | Light corn syrup |
| 8.5g | Salt |
| 6.9g | Xanthan gum |
| 6g | Pandan extract

## Tools

- Vitamix 5500
- Tamis mesh strainer (60-mesh)
- Milligram precision scale
- Food scale
- Stainless steel mixing bowls
- Stainless steel dim sum steamers
- Whynter ice cream maker

## Procedure

**Steam the rice.** Steam 685g Thai sticky rice until fully cooked and tender.

**Infuse.** Working in batches if needed, blend the hot rice with all 2970g coconut milk in the Vitamix until fully smooth.

**Strain.** Push the blended mixture through the tamis. Discard solids. Reserve the strained liquid.

**Emulsify.** Add 16g sunflower lecithin to the strained liquid in the Vitamix. Let hydrate 2 minutes. Stream in 242g coconut oil on low, then ramp to high for 30 seconds until opaque and homogeneous.

**Combine.** Whisk in 692g sugar, 346g corn syrup, 8.5g salt, and 6.9g xanthan gum until fully dissolved.

**Chill and churn.** Refrigerate below 4°C, at least 4 hours. Churn in 2 batches in the Whynter.
